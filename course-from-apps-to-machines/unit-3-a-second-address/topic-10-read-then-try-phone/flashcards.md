**Front:** In a URL, what is the scheme?

**Back:** The protocol the browser must use. In `http://localhost:8000/` it is `http`.

---

**Front:** In a URL, what is the host?

**Back:** Which machine is being asked. A domain name, or an IP address in that slot.

---

**Front:** Why must you type `:8000` in `http://localhost:8000/`?

**Back:** A port is the gate. Browsers hide port 80. 8000 is not that gate, so it stays in the address.

---

**Front:** After `curl -v`, what do `>` and `<` mark?

**Back:** `>` is the request you sent. `<` is the response the server sent.

---

**Front:** What does status 200 mean?

**Back:** The request succeeded.
