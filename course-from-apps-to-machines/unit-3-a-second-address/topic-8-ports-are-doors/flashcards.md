**Front:** What is a port?

**Back:** A number that picks which program on a machine should answer — the door.

---

**Front:** Usable TCP port range?

**Back:** 1–65535. Port 0 is reserved.

---

**Front:** What does port 80 usually mean?

**Back:** The usual door for `http://`.

---

**Front:** What does port 443 usually mean?

**Back:** The usual door for `https://`.

---

**Front:** What is port 8000 registered as?

**Back:** `irdmi`, not HTTP. Local servers use it by convention.
