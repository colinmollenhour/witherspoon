**Front:** What does `mkdir -p ~/projects/first-site` do?

**Back:** Creates the whole path. Makes parents as needed. No error if the folder already exists.

---

**Front:** What does `touch scratch.txt` do?

**Back:** Creates an empty file with that name.

---

**Front:** `cp a b` versus `mv a b` — which one leaves `a` in place?

**Back:** `cp` leaves the original. `mv` does not.

---

**Front:** What extra flag does `cp` need to copy a directory?

**Back:** `-r` (recursive). Plain `cp` is for files.

---

**Front:** Where does `rm scratch.txt` put the file?

**Back:** Nowhere. There is no trash and no undo.
