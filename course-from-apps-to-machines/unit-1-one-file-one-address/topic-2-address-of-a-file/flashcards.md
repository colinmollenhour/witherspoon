**Front:** How do you tell an absolute path from a relative one?

**Back:** Look at the first character. `/` means absolute. Anything else is relative.

---

**Front:** What does `~` stand for in a path?

**Back:** Your home folder. On Linux that is `/home/you`.

---

**Front:** What does `.` mean in a path?

**Back:** The folder you are standing in. From inside `first-site`, this page is `./index.html`.

---

**Front:** What does `..` mean in a path?

**Back:** The folder above the one you are standing in.

---

**Front:** Default Mac filesystem: does case matter in a filename?

**Back:** Not for identity. APFS is case-insensitive by default, and case-preserving — it remembers the capitals you typed.
